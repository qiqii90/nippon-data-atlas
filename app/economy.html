Economy

The world's fourth-largest economy

Japan produced $4.19 trillion of goods and services in 2024. Growth was nearly flat in real terms (+0.1%), and the dollar value fell because the yen weakened — in yen, nominal GDP rose for a fourth straight year.

Why the dollar figure fell: Japan's nominal GDP in yen rose from ¥637 trillion in 2024, but the yen averaged about 151 to the dollar, so the dollar figure dropped to $4.19 trillion from $4.38 trillion in 2023. The size of the economy did not shrink — the exchange rate moved.

What the economy makes

Services produce 70.3% of gross value added, industry 28.7% and agriculture 1.0% (2024). Jobs follow a similar split: 73.8% in services, 23.3% in industry, 2.9% in agriculture.

Trade: what Japan sells and buys

Japan exported $718 billion of goods and imported $752 billion in 2024 — a goods deficit of $34 billion. Vehicles and parts are the biggest export (21.7% of goods exports), ahead of machinery (19.1%) and electronics (18.5%).
