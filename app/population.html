Births and deaths

In 2023 Japan recorded 727,277 births and 1,590,503 deaths — a natural decrease of 863,226 people, the largest on record. The crude birth rate in 2024 was 5.7 per 1,000 people and the crude death rate 13.3 per 1,000.
