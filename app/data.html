Method notes

  • Years differ by figure on purpose. Each figure shows the newest year its source has published. A 2022 figure beside a 2024 figure is not stale data — it is the latest release.
  • Nominal GDP in dollars moves with the exchange rate. Japan's GDP in yen rose in 2024; in dollars it fell, because the yen averaged about 151 to the dollar. Both series are shown on the Economy page.
  • Prefecture populations are estimates for 1 October 2024 from the Statistics Bureau's Population Estimates, except in census years. Regional totals on the Regions page are sums of those prefecture estimates.
  • Gross enrolment can pass 100% because it counts pupils outside the official age band for the level. It is not an error.
  • Rounding. Shares are rounded to one decimal place, so listed shares can add to 99.9% or 100.1%. Population in millions is rounded to one decimal.
