# Data sources — Nippon Data Atlas

Every figure on the site comes from one of the publications below. Each
figure on the site names its source and the year it describes; the Data &
Sources page in the site lists them figure by figure.

## Publications used

- **World Bank, World Development Indicators (WDI)** — population, GDP
  (nominal and per capita), GDP per capita PPP, inflation, unemployment,
  labour force and participation, fertility, life expectancy, infant and
  under-5 mortality, physicians, health and education spending, enrolment,
  Gini index, homicides, R&D spending, researchers, age structure,
  population density, urban share, land area. Vintage: WDI release of 2026;
  latest year per indicator is 2024 unless the table says otherwise.
- **IMF, World Economic Outlook (April 2026)** — real GDP growth, 2015–2024.
- **Statistics Bureau of Japan, Ministry of Internal Affairs and
  Communications, Population Estimates** — all 47 prefecture populations as
  of 1 October 2024, and the national total of 123.80 million on that date.
  Regional totals on the Regions page are sums of these prefecture estimates.
- **Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, Vital Statistics 2023** —
  births 727,277 and deaths 1,590,503.
- **OECD Health Statistics / Health at a Glance 2025** — hospital beds
  (12.6 per 1,000, 2022) and doctor consultations (12.1 per person, 2023).
- **UNDP, Human Development Report 2023–24** — Human Development Index
  0.925, mean years of schooling 12.7, expected years of schooling 15.5.
- **WITS / World Bank (UN Comtrade)** — goods exports $718bn, goods imports
  $752bn, 2024; top export markets (US 20.2%, China 17.6%, South Korea 6.5%)
  and top import suppliers (China 22.4%, US 10.1%, Australia 8.6%).
- **UNSD / ILOSTAT, via the Destatis Japan country profile** — sector shares
  of gross value added (services 70.3%, industry 28.7%, agriculture 1.0%)
  and of employment (services 73.8%, industry 23.3%, agriculture 2.9%),
  2024.

## Computed figures (labelled as such on the site)

- Natural change 2023 (−863,226) = births minus deaths, MHLW figures.
- GDP per capita for a selected year = nominal GDP ÷ population, both from
  WDI. For 2024 this reproduces the published $33,797.
- Prefecture shares of Japan = prefecture population ÷ national total
  123.80 million, 1 October 2024.
- Regional totals and shares = sums of the prefecture estimates in that
  region; shares may not add to 100% because of rounding.

## Notes

- Years differ by figure on purpose: each figure shows the newest year its
  source has published.
- Nominal GDP in US dollars moves with the exchange rate. Japan's GDP in yen
  rose in 2024 (¥636.9 trillion); in dollars it fell to $4.19 trillion
  because the yen averaged about 151 to the dollar.
- Gross enrolment can pass 100% because it counts pupils outside the
  official age band for the level.
- No figure on this site is estimated by the atlas itself.
